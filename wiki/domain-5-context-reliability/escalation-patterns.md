# Escalation & Ambiguity Patterns

> Escalate for explicit human requests, policy gaps, or no progress — not sentiment or confidence.

## What it is
Deciding when an agent should hand off to a human or pause to ask,
versus continuing to solve.

## Why it matters
Bad triggers (negative sentiment, self-reported confidence) cause
both over- and under-escalation. The exam wants policy/workflow-based
triggers.

## Key ideas
- **Valid triggers**: explicit "I want a human", **policy silent/
  ambiguous**, workflow stuck (no progress).
- Explicit "human now" → escalate. Frustrated but solvable →
  acknowledge + solve. See [[escalate-vs-clarify-vs-solve]].
- **Don't** use sentiment as a proxy for complexity.
- **Don't** trust self-reported confidence.
- **Multiple customer matches** → ask for identifiers; don't guess.
- Policy doesn't cover the case → escalate; **don't invent a policy**.
- Escalation = a **structured handoff summary**, not a vague one.
