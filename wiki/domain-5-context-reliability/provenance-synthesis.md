# Provenance in Multi-Source Synthesis

> Every claim keeps its source, date, and excerpt; conflicts are annotated, not averaged.

## What it is
Preserving where each claim came from when combining multiple sources
into one synthesis.

## Why it matters
Summarization tends to drop citations and silently pick one value
when sources conflict — losing exactly the provenance a cited report
needs.

## Key ideas
- Use **claim-source mappings** — every claim carries its source.
- **Conflicting credible sources** → preserve **both** values and
  explain context; don't randomly pick one.
- Keep **publication/collection dates**; don't treat older vs newer
  as a contradiction without checking the time frame.
- Let the **coordinator reconcile conflicts** before synthesis (don't
  hide the conflict upstream).
- Format different content types appropriately (not one generic
  format).
